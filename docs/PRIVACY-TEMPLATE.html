Version: draft-2026-10-08. This is a preparation template, not a completed notice for collecting registrations. The team must confirm every highlighted item and publish matching language versions before activating signup.

Who is responsible?

TEAM TO CONFIRM: controller's full legal identity, establishment/address and valid privacy contact email. Confirm any representative or data protection officer if applicable.

What the future registration will collect

Adult email address, parent/carer or teacher/educator role, optional pilot interest, contact consent and its timestamp, accepted notice version, preferred language, registration identifier, creation timestamp and registration status. Do not submit children's personal data.

Why and on what basis?

Proposed purpose: contact interested adults about Lumi early access and future testing or feedback opportunities. Pilot interest is optional. No sale of the registration list, advertising profiles or unrelated marketing is proposed.

TEAM TO CONFIRM: precise purposes, communication scope and frequency, consent wording and appropriate legal basis (proposed: consent under GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Registration must remain voluntary. Any later research or child-facing product needs its own assessment and notice.

Retention and deletion

TEAM TO CONFIRM: an exact retention period or review deadline, who performs reviews, deletion process, backup expiry and any justified retention after withdrawal. No period has been invented.

Providers and transfers

The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. A future Supabase project will provide PostgreSQL and the submission endpoint; no project is connected yet. No analytics, advertising cookies, persistent visitor tracking, IP collection or mouse-history recording is implemented in this site's application. Pointer positions are used transiently on your device to animate letters and drag Lumi, and are not transmitted or stored.

Hosting/network providers receive technical connection information when serving requests and may maintain security logs independently. Simulation mode describes form entries, not an absence of all provider logging.

TEAM TO CONFIRM: contracts and roles for GitHub, Supabase and any future email provider; selected EU region; subprocessors, provider logging/retention, processing locations, international transfers and applicable safeguards. An EU database region alone does not establish that all processing stays in the EU.

Your rights and withdrawal

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and, where applicable, portability or objection. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it at any time, without affecting earlier lawful processing. You may complain to the competent data protection supervisory authority.

TEAM TO CONFIRM: working privacy email and an easy, free withdrawal/deletion route, identity verification proportionate to the request, response handling and relevant supervisory authority/contact details. Do not activate collection until requests can actually be handled.

Security and access

The planned database denies public reading, listing, updating and deleting. Only the validated backend inserts registrations; authorized administrators use the Supabase dashboard. Exports must be stored privately and deleted when no longer needed.
