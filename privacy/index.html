Your privacy matters.

Early-access registration is now open. We collect only the adult registration details shown on the form: email, role, optional pilot interest, contact consent, consent time, language, notice version and registration status. We do not collect children's data, IP addresses, location, device fingerprints, browsing history or mouse movements.

This website uses no analytics or advertising cookies. Lumi's interactive animations run locally in your browser; pointer positions are not stored or sent to us. GitHub Pages serves the website and Supabase stores registrations in an EU (Ireland) project. Both providers may process technical connection data under their own service policies.

Purpose and choices

We use the registration to contact adults about Lumi early access and optional pilot or feedback opportunities. Pilot interest is optional. Contact consent is required for registration and can be withdrawn by contacting the Lumi team; requests for deletion will be handled through the authorized Supabase dashboard.

Security

Public visitors cannot read, list, update or delete registration rows. A server-only Edge Function validates submissions and writes through a restricted database function. Registration records are not placed in the public website bundle or redirect URL.
