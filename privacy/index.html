Privacy information · current release status

Registration is not active in this published build. Do not enter real personal information until the controller details, retention period, withdrawal route, processor disclosures and child-use safeguards have been confirmed. The server has independent activation gates.

Public interactive demo

When you explicitly enable the camera, video is processed locally in your browser by the open-source MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite model. The site does not upload or save camera video, frames, pose landmarks or inferred actions. If camera access is denied or the model is unavailable, movement play can continue in clearly labelled unverified manual mode.

When you explicitly tap TALK, microphone audio is streamed to the configured ElevenLabs conversational agent so it can respond. The website does not intentionally save recordings. ElevenLabs is a separate service provider; its agent configuration, retention and processing locations must be checked before public activation. Do not claim that all processing is on-device.

Early Access registration

If activated after the required review, the secure backend accepts only adult email address, parent/carer or educator role, optional pilot interest, contact consent and timestamp, preferred language, privacy notice version, registration time and status. Do not submit children's names, ages, photographs or other unnecessary information. Supabase remains the authoritative database. A private team Google Sheet may receive confirmed records through a server-side outbox and retry process; it is not public.

Before collection

The team must confirm the controller's legal identity and contact, purposes and legal basis, retention and deletion process, providers and transfers, rights request route, any representative or data protection officer, and safeguards for child-facing use. See the activation checklist.
