The €235,000 plan comprises €141,750 personnel, €45,000 technology, €25,000 legal/safety/IP and €23,250 pilots/administration. We remove the whole €11,500 voice/cloud envelope from technology, then add modelled delivery costs once. €15,000 reserve is not an expense. Contribution excludes payroll and corporate costs. The operating illustration includes the remaining budget and any entered scale overhead; excludes financing, corporate income tax, refunds, churn and unbudgeted acquisition/support. This cash-oriented plan is not accounting EBIT. The cohort threshold is technical break-even, not company break-even. At scale, headcount, acquisition and support need a new budget. Doubling usage is a sensitivity test, not a usage entitlement.
